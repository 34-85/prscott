# Privacy Policy — PSMF Tracker

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

PSMF Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") helps you track weight, meals, and water
and see PSMF-style diet trends. This policy explains what data the app handles,
where it lives, and the choices you have. It is written to describe how the app
works today and how the optional account, Apple Health, and AI features work
when you turn them on.

The app is a tracking tool, **not medical advice** — see the in-app Health &
Safety disclaimer.

## The short version

- Your tracking data is **stored on your device by default.** We do not run
  servers that hold it unless you create an account and turn on sync.
- We **do not sell your data**, and we **do not use it for advertising.**
- Optional features (an account, Apple Health, the AI Coach) each share data
  only when *you* enable them, and only as described below.
- You can **export or delete** your data at any time.

## What the app handles

**Data you enter.** Morning weight, meals and their estimated macros, water,
notes, day types, plan settings (goal, target weeks, targets), and any personal
foods you add.

**Account data (only if you create one).** Your email address, used to sign you
in and sync your data across your devices and the web.

**Apple Health (only if you connect it).** With your permission, the app can
read your latest body-weight to fill in your morning weigh-in. Health data is
used only inside the app to show your trends; it is **never** used for
advertising and is **never** sold or shared for anyone else's use.

**AI Coach (only if you enable it).** The AI Coach is optional and uses a key
*you* provide ("bring your own key"). When you send a message, the app sends
your message, your API key, and a short summary of your recent tracking data
directly from your device to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to generate a reply.
Your key is stored only in your browser/app on your device — never on our
servers. This feature is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

**Technical data.** If we add crash reporting or basic, privacy-respecting
analytics to improve reliability, we will update this policy and describe what
is collected before it is turned on.

## Where your data is stored

- **Today:** on your device, in local app storage (with a local backup inside
  the app on iOS so you don't lose data). It does not leave your device except
  through the optional features above.
- **With an account (when available):** your data syncs to your own private
  space in our hosting provider's database (Supabase). It is isolated per
  account by row-level security, so no other user can read it, and it is
  encrypted in transit.

## How we use your data

Only to provide the app's features: recording your entries, computing macros
and compliance, showing trends and coaching, and — if you enable them — syncing
across your devices and answering AI Coach questions. That's it. We do not sell
your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## Who your data is shared with

We don't share your data with third parties for their own purposes. The only
processors involved, and only when you use the relevant feature, are:

- **Anthropic** — if you enable the AI Coach (your messages + key + data summary
  go to Anthropic to generate replies).
- **Supabase** — if you create an account (hosts your synced data).
- **Apple** — the App Store, and Apple Health if you connect it, under Apple's
  terms.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the app and its users.

## Your choices and rights

- **Use it without an account** — everything works on-device.
- **Export your data** — download your entries at any time.
- **Delete your data** — clear it on-device, and (with an account) delete your
  account and all associated data from within the app.
- **Disconnect Apple Health** or **remove your AI key** at any time in Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, under
GDPR or CCPA) to access, correct, or delete your personal data and to object to
certain processing. Contact us and we'll help.

## Data retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or remove the app. Account data (when
available) is kept until you delete your account, after which it is removed from
our systems.

## Security

Your data is encrypted in transit. With an account, per-user row-level security
isolates your data in the database. Your AI key stays on your device. No system
is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and minimize what
we hold.

## Children

PSMF Tracker is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Please do
not use it, or provide any information, if you are under 17.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, when the account,
Health, or AI features ship). We'll change the "Last updated" date above and,
for material changes, note them in the app.
