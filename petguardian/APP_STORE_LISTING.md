## Description (max 4000)

If something happens to you, who takes care of your pet, and who pays for it?

A pet is legally property, not an heir. You can't leave money directly to your
dog or cat, and a will alone runs through probate, which can take months while
your animal needs food, medication, and a home right now. Every U.S. state and
Washington, D.C. now allow a pet trust that solves this. PetGuardian helps you
build one, step by step, in about twenty minutes.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Name a primary caregiver and a backup, so there's never a gap in who takes
  your pet.
- Add a separate trustee to hold and control the money, so care is funded and
  overseen by someone other than the caregiver.
- Record everything a caregiver needs: vet and emergency vet, microchip,
  medications, allergies, diet, routine, and behavior.
- See a live readiness score that shows exactly what's still missing.
- Generate attorney-ready documents: an Animal Care Trust Directive, a Pet Care
  Memorandum, and an Emergency Wallet Card.
- Keep an offline Emergency Card on your phone, ready with no signal and no login.
- Get a yearly reminder to review your plan as life changes.

WORKS IN ALL 50 STATES + D.C.
PetGuardian tailors your plan to your state's pet-trust law, including the
statute, how long the trust can last, and who can enforce it.

BUILT FOR TRUST
Your plan and the people in it are sensitive. We use encrypted connections, we
never sell your personal information, and you can permanently delete your account
and all of your data at any time from inside the app.

IMPORTANT
PetGuardian is a self-help planning tool. It is not a law firm and does not
provide legal advice. The documents it creates are drafts meant for review with
a licensed attorney in your state before you sign or fund them.
