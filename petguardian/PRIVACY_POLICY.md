# PetGuardian Privacy Policy

**Effective: August 22, 2026**

PetGuardian ("we," "us") helps pet owners create a funded, state-specific care
plan for their animals. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and
the choices you have. PetGuardian is a self-help planning tool, not a law firm,
and does not provide legal advice.

The authoritative, user-facing version of this policy is published in the app at
`/privacy`. This file is the repository copy.

## Information we collect

We collect only what you provide to create your plan:

- **Account:** your name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, and state.
- **Plan content:** details about your pets, chosen caregivers and trustees (their names and contact details, which you provide), funding sources and amounts, and your care and disposition instructions.
- **Technical:** basic server logs needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and the service is intended for adults.

## How we use your information

We use your information solely to provide the service: to save your plan, generate
your documents (the trust directive, care memorandum, and emergency card), and let
you return to and update your plan. We do not use your plan content for advertising.

## How your information is stored and protected

Your data is stored in a secured database and transmitted over encrypted
connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is protected by authentication. No method of
storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to
protect your information.

## Sharing, referrals, and affiliates

We do not sell your personal information. If we offer optional referrals to third
parties (for example pet insurance or attorney review), those are shown only with
your action and we clearly disclose any relationship where we may earn a fee. We
share your details with a third party only when you choose to proceed.

## Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from **Account → Delete
account** inside the app. Deleting your account immediately and permanently
removes your profile and all associated plans, pets, caregivers, trustees, and
funding records. This cannot be undone. You may also contact us to request
deletion or a copy of your data.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict use of your personal information, and to opt out of any sale or sharing.
We honor a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request; because we do
not sell personal information, this is our default posture.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective
date at the top.
